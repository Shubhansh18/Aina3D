Seamless Experience

How Aina Works?

AINA lives quietly behind your product pages as a lightweight plugin. Customers just tap “Find My Size with AINA”—the rest happens in a smooth five-step flow.

Start Your Profile

Shoppers select gender and enter a few basics like height and weight.

1
2

Upload Guided Images

Users upload front and side-profile body images plus a clear face photo, which AINA then processes through a secure, privacy-safe flow for AI analysis.

AI Measurement Extraction & Review

AINA extracts and displays key measurements—including chest, waist, hips, shoulders, and inseam—allowing shoppers to review and fine-tune any detail for total accuracy.

3
4

Digital Twin Generation

Once confirmed, AINA generates a proportionally accurate AI digital twin based on the final body profile — creating a reusable, personalized trial identity.

Smart Size & Fit Intelligence

AINA recommends the ideal size and fit—snug, regular, or relaxed—based on your brand's unique charts, saving the shopper's profile for an effortless, confident experience across all future visits.

About Aina

The New Language
of Fit.

We started Aina when one thing became impossible to ignore: online sizing was a guessing game.

Buying clothes online meant ordering three sizes to keep one, frustrated shoppers and bleeding brand profits. It became clear: someone needed to solve this. That “someone” ended up being us.

"Four like-minded individuals, passionate about fashion & tech, met on an online course. Casual conversations quickly turned into a shared mission."

Our team brings together years of experience across styling, design, 3D modeling, and full-stack development. We know exactly where shoppers struggle, what brands need, and how technology can bridge that gap.
